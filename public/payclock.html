Calculations match the existing Payclock Excel exporter

Hours and payroll: calculations use the enabled timestamps in chronological order. Four punches form two work intervals; the gap between them is lunch. Two punches use the first-to-last interval with no lunch deduction. Odd or extra punch counts use consecutive pairs and remain provisional for review. Weekdays have up to 8 regular hours, with the remainder as overtime; all weekend hours are overtime. The existing exporter’s payroll minute conversion and rounding are retained.

Original records and corrections: clock punches stay unchanged in the raw cache. Saved additions, time changes, assignments and excluded punches are held in a separate shared correction layer. Reports, employee statistics and Excel use the resulting enabled timestamps. Each save and undo records who made the change and when; a reason is optional.

Suggestions are drafts: employee history helps suggest punch assignments. Unchecking Include makes that punch Unassigned and reassigns the remaining enabled punches. Add missing punch inserts a blank field in the proposed missing slot; it never guesses the time. Enter the actual time, check the assignments, and press Save to apply the correction. Unsaved drafts do not change reports or exports.

Needs Review: unresolved cases remain provisional and their calculated hours are included in totals, so review them before using the report for payroll. Saved corrections stay in the list with a blue Corrected label, even when their punches now calculate successfully. Unresolved-day counts remain separate from corrected rows. Click a review pattern to see its matching days.

Excel downloads: Download this table as Excel includes every row matching the selected report sheet, period, team, employee and applicable day filter, in the current sort order—not just the visible page. Saved corrections are included; the review sheet retains corrected rows with a blue highlight. Reload a report if another user has changed its corrections before exporting.

Coverage and shared updates: complete cached days are reused; missing or partial days are fetched for all employees. Today remains partial until a later-day read. Days without punches are not treated as absences. Other users’ open reports pick up saved corrections automatically; stale drafts are rejected if local corrections or original clock punches changed while the editor was open.
