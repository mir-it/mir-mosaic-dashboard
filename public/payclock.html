Calculations match the existing Payclock Excel exporter

Four punches: first work interval + second work interval; the middle interval is lunch. Two punches: first-to-last work interval, with no lunch deduction. Odd or extra punch counts use consecutive pairs and are flagged for review. All punches, including extras, are visible in day details.

The exporter splits weekdays into up to 8 regular hours and the remainder as overtime; all weekend hours are overtime. Its payroll minute-conversion chart and rounding are preserved. These are the existing application’s rules. Review values remain provisional and are included in totals, as in the workbook.

Columns and punch placement match Excel exactly. For a two-punch day, the second timestamp appears in the workbook’s “Lunch Out” column even though it is the last punch of the day. A missing value stays blank; no attendance is invented for days with no punches.
